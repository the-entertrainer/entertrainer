# Editorial Workspace Preloader

The alternate preloader removes the signal mark and route line entirely. It is a small tabletop animation in which a folded paper page lands, a cobalt highlighter passes across a line of text, and a clipped note card settles into the upper corner. The assembled composition becomes a finished learning card before the application takes over.

The sequence is finite: paper at 0–360ms, highlighter at 260–740ms, note card at 560–920ms, wordmark at 760–1100ms. It does not loop or imply a measurable loading percentage. Under reduced motion, the completed paper card, highlight, note card, and wordmark are visible immediately.
