# Shockwave Compact Logo Plan

## Decision

The selected **Shockwave Type** wordmark remains the primary identity. Its core idea is simple: a black lowercase wordmark crosses a warm-yellow field of concentric circles. The circles represent attention narrowing around one useful idea, not “hypnosis” as a product claim. The preloader is the expressive use of that idea; the compact logo must be quieter and work at very small sizes.

> **Primary wordmark:** `entertrainer` in black over warm yellow concentric circles.

## Compact Mark Concept

The compact mark will combine a lowercase **e** with three yellow rings. A solid black lowercase `e` sits in the middle, while the rings are interrupted behind its counter and horizontal stroke. This makes the mark read as both an `e` and a focused ripple without needing a separate icon beside it.

| Element | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Letterform | Heavy, open lowercase `e` in black | It remains recognisable at favicon size and links directly to Entertrainer. |
| Rings | Three warm-yellow concentric circles | Three is enough to recall the selected wordmark without producing visual noise. |
| Interruption | Rings disappear behind the `e`, not through it | The `e` remains the subject rather than becoming an illegible pattern. |
| Background | Cream by default; black for inverse uses | It preserves the paper-and-ink character of the selected direction. |
| Accent yellow | `#FFD43B` as the starting brand yellow | It is vivid against ink and cream without using an acidic neon yellow. |

## Intended Uses

The compact mark will be used only where the full wordmark would become too small: the browser favicon, a social profile avatar, the mobile masthead, app-like tool headers, and the preloader’s first frame. The full Shockwave Type wordmark stays on the desktop masthead, page headers, motion end-frame, and promotional artwork.

## Motion Rule

The mark will have one motion behaviour: rings expand once from the `e` and settle. It will not spin continuously. In reduced-motion mode, the final three-ring mark appears immediately. This keeps the idea recognisable without turning the logo into background scenery.

## Build Sequence After Approval

1. Draw the compact mark as a clean vector asset in cream, black, and yellow versions.
2. Test it at favicon, mobile-masthead, and social-avatar sizes before changing the site header.
3. Create a matching animated inline-SVG version with the single outward ring sequence.
4. Update the selected wordmark, favicon, preloader, and yellow accent tokens as one coordinated identity release.
5. Revise supporting artwork only after the compact mark and core palette are approved, so the work follows a stable identity rather than a temporary colour swap.

## What This Mark Will Not Be

The compact mark will not become a generic target, eye, spiral, light bulb, or hypnosis icon. It will not use gradients, violet remnants, glass effects, or an unrelated mascot. The `e` must remain readable before the viewer notices the circles.
