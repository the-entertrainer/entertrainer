# Visual Explanation Expansion — From No AI to Know AI

## Selection rule

The course already contains a history image, a model-landscape image, a labelled graphic, diagrams, and several activities. This expansion will not add a visual after every paragraph. It will convert only explanations that ask a learner to mentally hold **several relationships at once**: a process across time, a contrast between stages, a changing set of choices, or a boundary between safe and consequential use.

The instructional prose remains before each visual. Each new visual acts as a compact second representation of the explanation, then hands the learner back to the existing worked example or purposeful activity.

## Selected visual explanations

| Lesson | Text-heavy concept | Visual block | Learning job | What remains as prose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 02 — What AI is | Task, information, pattern, output, and checking are connected parts of one use case. | **AI task canvas:** a five-stage route through the existing travel-time example. | Makes the task-to-check relationship visible in one glance. | Why an estimate is useful but not a promise, and why human judgement stays necessary. |
| 03 — Learning patterns | Training and inference happen at different times and examples shape later performance. | **Learn / use contrast diagram:** two connected lanes from labelled examples to a learned pattern to a new request and candidate output. | Separates the two stages and makes the example-to-output relationship concrete. | Why missing or biased examples create uncertainty and need testing. |
| 04 — Prediction engine | Context changes the distribution of likely next tokens; a response grows through repeated estimates. | **Context-weighting graphic:** three context tokens feed a small ranked-choice meter before the selected token joins the response. | Turns an abstract probability explanation into a visible before-and-after sequence. | Why plausible wording is not a verified fact. |
| 05 — Modern AI types | A single operational goal can call on several specialised systems. | **Delivery workflow illustration:** a quiet editorial scene showing a package, image, message, and operational tool as complementary inputs. | Reinforces that AI families support different jobs rather than competing as one general intelligence. | The existing labelled graphic and sorting game will retain the precise family definitions. |
| 07 — Know AI | Lower-risk and consequential uses require different evidence, control, and review. | **Use-with-care boundary graphic:** a balanced review desk with a public draft, protected information, evidence check, and named reviewer. | Makes the transition from capability to accountable use memorable before the scenario. | The existing four-question routine and scenario remain the source of procedural detail. |

## Deliberate exclusions

The history lesson already has an editorial timeline plus interactive timeline and ordering retrieval. The models lesson already has a model-landscape image, documented-capability text, and matching activity. The knowledge check and bonus memory round are retrieval moments, not explanatory screens, so new decoration would dilute their purpose.

All generated illustrations will be text-free, wide, calm editorial compositions in the established blue, white, charcoal, and muted warm-neutral visual family. The structured explanatory diagrams will be built in the course itself so their labels remain accurate, selectable, responsive, and available to assistive technology.
