# Compact Route Atlas homepage

## Three possible directions

| Theme name | Very brief intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| Quiet index | A near-monochrome publication index with one small colour signal per section. It would feel calm, selective, and editorial. | 0.07 |
| Route card | A compact, map-like landing page where one route image, three clear directions, and a short work list do all the orienting. | 0.04 |
| Studio shelf | A warm gallery shelf that gives equal visual weight to a few featured artefacts and lets the masthead carry navigation. | 0.09 |

## Chosen direction: Route card

**Design movement:** Swiss editorial minimalism, softened by the existing handmade Route Atlas signal. The page should behave like a concise front cover, not a full catalogue.

**Core principles:** The homepage has one job per section; navigation labels are not repeated as large content blocks; imagery appears only where it contributes orientation; and movement is reserved for the opening signal, subtle map tiles, and interaction feedback.

**Color philosophy:** White or near-black paper comes from the active theme. Violet-to-blue remains the single recognisable route signal, while yellow is omitted from the opening page to reduce visual competition.

**Layout paradigm:** A short vertical sequence: statement and map, three practical directions, two selected works. The footer contains the closing identity and contact route, eliminating a redundant dedicated closing section.

**Signature elements:** The five-bar signal, one cropped route-field image, and small paper tiles that drift only within the hero map.

**Interaction philosophy:** Links shift a few pixels and reveal their route arrow on hover or focus. The page never hides essential information behind a motion trigger.

**Animation:** The hero settles once on entry. Hero tiles float slowly within a small range; lists use reversible hover transitions. Reduced motion freezes decorative movement and leaves all content immediately visible.

**Typography system:** Fraunces creates the single large statement; Archivo carries navigation and labels; Source Serif 4 supports the short explanatory paragraphs. The page uses a small number of sizes and avoids oversized section headings.

**Brand essence:** Entertrainer makes complicated work easier to understand through practical lessons, projects, and tools. **Clear, grounded, curious.**

**Brand voice:** Direct and human, using plain action-led language. Example lines: “Choose a place to begin.” and “See a few things I made for real work.”

**Wordmark & logo:** The five-bar signal remains the recognisable mark. It lands in sequence during loading and stays still while readers navigate.

**Signature brand color:** Route violet, `#6958EA` in light mode and `#A29CFF` in dark mode.
