Original ~9s studio recording. Shipped copy is trimmed to the soft strike
and early ring (~1.4s), warmed in the low mids, gently rolled off on top,
and loudness-normalized quietly (~−22 LUFS) so it reads as a subtle deep
metallic ring under the sky ↔ metal dial flip — not a paper click, not a tick.

Plays once per flip only.
