Original GLB models are bundled locally. Runtime scaling and placement adapt them to the game scene. No login, CDN or external asset requests are required.

The roaches, turret rigs and remaining kitchen geometry are original procedural meshes, not third-party CC0 assets. Window shafts use a world-space, depth-clipped, shadow-occluded single-scattering ray march. Low/medium/high modes vary sampling and resolution; this is a real-time approximation, not offline path tracing.
