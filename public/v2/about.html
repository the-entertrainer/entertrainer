From housekeeping to course design

Four places, each one taught me something I now put into training.

  1. Club Mahindra, Coorg: housekeeping

    Cleaning rooms showed me what a new joiner has to learn in the first hour, and what gets skipped.

  2. The SEWA Chronicles: a comic

    Service lessons told as stories, checked line by line with the staff they were about.

  3. Marriott, Mahabaleshwar: learning and development

    Onboarding, the CDT Masterclass for department trainers, and the Ignite events.

  4. Concentrix, then Entertrainer

    Contact-centre training, and now essays, apps and games for anyone who trains people.
